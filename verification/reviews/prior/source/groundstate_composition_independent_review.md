## Mathematical checks

1. `gaussianL2Average_graph_energy_le_form` starts from the actual all-L2 Gaussian H1 graph-energy theorem and the proved equality of the Bochner average with the actual free heat operator. Expanding `||f-Q_h f||^2 >= 0` gives the exact form bound. Division uses `h > 0`. No gradient, integration-by-parts or desired energy conclusion is an input.
2. `gaussianL2Average_form_sequence_close` uses the actual bound `||Q_(h_k) f_k-f_k||^2 <= h_k E_(h_k)(f_k)`. The positive steps tend to zero and the real upper bounds converge to a finite real number, so their product tends to zero. The square-root argument proves strong L2 proximity even though the inputs vary with `k`; fixed-input strong continuity is not substituted here.
3. `gaussian_supported_normalized_minimizing_limit` constructs the actual H1 approximants itself. Gaussian contraction, exact normalization and the convergent energy upper bounds provide a uniform H1 bound. Compact localization extracts a strong L2 subsequence, while the energy passes through the original global Gaussian graphs. Consequently no cutoff-gradient constant or dimension factor enters the energy limit. The imported supported variational theorem proves actual H0 membership and actual Rayleigh-infimum attainment. Its finite-before-`toReal` step uses nonempty openness, already implied by the explicit origin hypothesis; dimension zero is allowed.
4. `gaussian_logConcave_groundstate_of_competitors` derives the actual normalized first weak H0 eigenfunction and strong L2 subsequence. It then applies the separately proved L2 log-concave representative theorem to the specified measurable real functions along exactly that subsequence. This supplies a globally finite nonnegative even pointwise log-concave representative almost everywhere equal to the actual value class. The proof does not assign pointwise log-concavity to an arbitrary Lp representative or use the unproved equivalence with a set-based notion of log-concave measure.
5. `exists_even_logConcave_dirichlet_groundstate` discharges the entire competitor-family input. A nonempty convex symmetric open domain contains the origin by averaging a point with its negative; boundedness supplies finite volume. The previous actual H0 attainment theorem supplies an energy competitor without assuming its log-concavity. Literal normalized finite Gaussian-killing iterates give the specified finite measurable even log-concave functions. Their exact L2 representative identity, norm one and zero exterior are established before the limit theorem is applied. The actual form bound is `lambda(U).toReal + 1/(j+1)`, with positive time step tending to zero. No eigenfunction simplicity, strict positivity, smooth boundary, spectral convergence or desired log-concave eigenfunction is assumed.

The final conclusion concerns one actual normalized first weak H0 groundstate and an explicit finite nonnegative even log-concave representative. It does not establish strict positivity throughout the interior, classical regularity, simplicity, uniqueness, log-concavity of every first eigenfunction, or Milman's separate existence assertion.
